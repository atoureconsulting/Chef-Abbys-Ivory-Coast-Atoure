## 2. Côte d'Ivoire: angles that are new and current

Each of these is timely in October 2026 and gives a "why now" beyond the food itself.

### A. Cocoa → chocolate (the supply chain Côte d'Ivoire owns)
- Côte d'Ivoire is the **world's #1 cocoa producer** (~40% of global supply), yet very little chocolate is eaten or made locally. That paradox makes a strong story.
- **New:** since **1 September 2026**, the **producer card (carte du producteur)** is mandatory for every cocoa and coffee transaction, under the national traceability system run by the Conseil du Café-Cacao. It prepares the sector for the **EU deforestation regulation (1 January 2027)**, and ~600 cooperatives are already fully traced. The 2026-27 main crop campaign opens in October, so her visit falls in harvest season.
- **Local hero:** **Axel-Emmanuel Gbaou, *Le Chocolatier Ivoirien*.** He is a former banker who has made Ivorian chocolate since 2011 and runs a lab in Cocody. His chocolate was named "best in the world" at the 2022 International Agricultural Show, and he has won medals at the Salon du Chocolat in Paris. He has trained **2,000+ women** in cocoa regions, for example in the village of **Abbé-Bégnini**, north of Abidjan. He already runs bean-to-bar workshops.

### B. Attiéké des Lagunes: now a protected-origin product run by women
- **Attiéké des Lagunes** received a **protected geographical indication (IGP)**. It went to market on **26 March 2026 in Dabou**.
- The producers' group covers **167 villages and ~16,000 women**, making **100,000+ tonnes a year worth ~100 billion FCFA**. The zone covers Abidjan, Dabou, Jacqueville and Grand-Lahou, and the group is headquartered in Dabou.
- ➜ This gives us the female-empowerment angle **and** the supply-chain angle in one location, about an hour from Abidjan. It also completes her earlier attiéké content instead of repeating it.

### C. School meals and youth
- The State is building **31 women-led production hubs** to supply school canteens with local food. WFP and KOICA launched a local-produce school-feeding programme in the north in April 2026.
- **Fondation Magic System** (A'Salfo) builds schools and announced **5 new schools in 2026**, one of them in **Jacqueville**, which is also in the attiéké IGP zone. That makes a possible Day-2 link: women producers → school lunch.
- **Fondation Children of Africa** (First Lady Dominique Ouattara) works on children, education and health. It is a high-level option, but slow to reach.

### D. The government is already pushing gastronomy
- The **4th Festival de la Cuisine Ivoirienne (FCI)** takes place **16–17 October 2026 in Kong**, run by the **Ministry of Tourism & Leisure** (Minister **Siandou Fofana**, to be verified at time of outreach). Its theme is *"Ivorian cuisine: cultural heritage and a lever for tourism"*. It has 26 finalists from 13 regions.
- ➜ This is the ministry hook. The ministry has just run a national cuisine campaign, and a TIME-100 creator arriving 48 hours later lets it amplify that work internationally. **The FCI laureates can cook with her.**

## 3. Practical facts for logistics

- **Languages:** French is official. She works in English, so a **bilingual fixer/interpreter is essential**, on camera as well.
- **Currency:** West African CFA franc (XOF), pegged at **€1 = 655.957 XOF**. The budget uses **$1 ≈ 565 XOF** (check the rate on the day).
- **Visas:** Ghanaian and other **ECOWAS** passport holders do not need a visa. Crew from outside ECOWAS need an **e-visa** (apply online a few days before; allow a week).
- **Weather:** mid-to-late October is Abidjan's **short rainy season**, so every outdoor segment needs a covered backup.
- **Accra → Abidjan:** ~1 h flight (several daily carriers) or ~9–11 h by road via the Elubo/Noé border.
- **Abidjan → Monrovia:** **Air Côte d'Ivoire flies nonstop** (~1 h 30). Published schedules conflict (one source lists Sun/Tue/Fri); **check the days for 22 October** before fixing the itinerary. **Road is not recommended.** It is ~900 km through the far west (Man → Danané → Ganta → Monrovia), takes 2 days, and would cost her a full content day.
- **Drones:** filming with drones needs authorisation from the civil aviation authority (**ANAC**). Our budget uses a local licensed operator.
