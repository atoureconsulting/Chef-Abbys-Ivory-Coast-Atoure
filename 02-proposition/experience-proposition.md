# Chef Abbys × Côte d'Ivoire: *Who Feeds Côte d'Ivoire?*

**Experience proposition · DRAFT v0.1 for discussion**
AToure Management & Consulting · for Chef Abbys' management · 23 September 2026
Programme dates: **Monday 19 – Wednesday 21 October 2026** (departure to Monrovia on Thursday 22 October)

---

## 1. The idea in one line

Chef Abbys has already *tasted* Abidjan. This time she meets **the people who feed Côte d'Ivoire**: the cocoa growers and chocolatiers, the 16,000 women behind *Attiéké des Lagunes*, the children at a school lunch, and the chefs and stars who carry Ivorian food to the world.

Her last visit was street food and learning attiéké. This stop goes **upstream (where food comes from)**, **inward (who it feeds)** and **outward (who celebrates it)**. It is the most *educational* stop on the West Africa Food Tour, and three days after her **Big Street Feast (16 October, Accra & Kumasi)** it becomes a natural continuation of her Zero Hunger cause.

## 2. Why Côte d'Ivoire, why now

| Hook | Why it works on camera |
|---|---|
| **World #1 in cocoa (~40% of global supply)**, yet few Ivorians eat Ivorian chocolate | A paradox that makes strong short-form content, and it is **harvest season** (the 2026-27 main crop opens in October) |
| **Cocoa traceability became mandatory on 1 Sept 2026** (producer card, ahead of the EU deforestation law in Jan 2027) | Gives her audience a timely, educational "why this matters" |
| **Attiéké des Lagunes won protected-origin status**, launched in Dabou in March 2026. The group is **16,000 women in 167 villages**, producing a ~100 bn FCFA industry | Women's economic power plus food heritage, and it completes her earlier attiéké content instead of repeating it |
| **The Festival de la Cuisine Ivoirienne** (Ministry of Tourism) ends in Kong on **17 Oct**, two days before she lands | The ministry has a fresh national campaign to amplify, and **the newly crowned laureates can cook with her** |
| **The Ghana–Côte d'Ivoire rivalry** (football, attiéké vs gari, "whose jollof…") | A friendly, built-in engagement driver for both audiences |

## 3. The three days

Each day is **one episode** with its own story arc. Every day includes a free block for her team's own filming.

### Day 1 · Monday 19 Oct: **"The Bean"** · Cocoa → Chocolate

| Time | Moment | Content value |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival | **Protocol welcome at Félix-Houphouët-Boigny Airport.** Fast-track, then a pagne-wrapped welcome kit and fresh *bissap* and *gnamakoudji* (ginger) juice on the kerb. | "I've landed" opener |
| Late morning | Check-in, rest, crew prep | — |
| Afternoon | **Bean-to-bar at *Le Chocolatier Ivoirien*** (Cocody) with **Axel-Emmanuel Gbaou**, the ex-banker whose chocolate was named "best in the world" (2022) and who has trained 2,000+ women in cocoa villages. Pod → fermentation → bar. She makes a Ghana-meets-Côte d'Ivoire bar (Ghana is cocoa #2). | Educational hero piece: "Why the world's cocoa capital barely eats chocolate" |
| Evening | **Welcome dinner with a leading Ivorian chef.** Our first choice is **Charlie Koffi (Villa Alfira)**, who reinterprets heritage dishes such as *gouagouassou*. Around 8 guests: tourism and culture stakeholders, a food creator, a foundation lead. | High-profile meetings (a stated tour goal) |

*Upgrade:* swap the late-morning rest for a **cocoa farm visit** in Azaguié or Abbé-Bégnini (~1 h), so the chain runs *farm → bar* on the same day.

### Day 2 · Tuesday 20 Oct: **"The Lagoon"** · Attiéké, women and a school lunch

| Time | Moment | Content value |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Drive west along the lagoon to **Dabou** (~1 h) | Scenic B-roll, drone |
| Morning | **The women of *Attiéké des Lagunes***, the protected-origin producers' HQ. Cassava → pressing → fermenting → steaming, the economics, and the label that fights fakes. She cooks alongside them as a student, not a demonstrator. | Female empowerment + supply chain + heritage |
| Midday | **"From the lagoon to the lunchbox."** Chef Abbys, the attiéké women and the school cooks prepare **lunch for ~400 pupils** at a partner school in the lagoon zone. Our target is a **Fondation Magic System** school (one is due in **Jacqueville** in 2026); a school feeding programme school is the alternative. Short talk: *food as a career*. | Her Zero Hunger cause, in Côte d'Ivoire; the emotional peak of the stop |
| Afternoon | Return to Abidjan via a **market supply stop** (Adjamé or Treichville): *where does Abidjan's food come from?* | Context, fast cuts |
| Evening | **Maquis night.** Garba, alloco, braised fish, live music (coupé-décalé or zouglou) in Yopougon or Marcory with local food creators. | The fun, shareable episode ending |

### Day 3 · Wednesday 21 Oct: **"The Table"** · Culture, stars and the cook-off

| Time | Moment | Content value |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | **Musée des Civilisations de Côte d'Ivoire** (Plateau), private guided visit: food rituals, masks, the Akan–Ghana link | Cultural depth; shows the two countries' shared roots |
| Late morning | **Courtesy call at the Ministry of Tourism & Leisure** (or the Culture / Women & Family ministries), linked to the Festival de la Cuisine Ivoirienne | Official endorsement; opens the door to co-funding |
| Afternoon | ⭐ **"Ghana × Côte d'Ivoire: the Cook-Off."** Lagoon-view venue. **Chef Abbys + the FCI 2026 laureates + a celebrity guest** (targets: **A'Salfo of Magic System**, with **Didier Drogba** as the stretch ask) and **2–3 Ivorian food creators** (e.g. **@afrofoodie Yasmine Fofana**, Ebenezer, Prince Edja). Friendly rivalry: *attiéké vs gari*, *kedjenou vs light soup*, with the celebrity judging. | Headline social moment, reaching both countries' audiences and a celebrity's |
| Evening | *Upgrade:* **sunset pinasse (boat) on the Ébrié lagoon**, farewell toast | Closing shot of the Abidjan skyline |

**Thursday 22 Oct:** airport protocol, then **Air Côte d'Ivoire nonstop to Monrovia** (~1 h 30). The flight days still need confirming.

## 6. Sponsorship reality (from the call)

- **Ministries and large sponsors are slow**, and many **2026 budgets are already committed**. With 4 weeks' lead time, **ask for in-kind support first** and cash second.
- The quickest decisions come from **private brands with a local marketing lead** (mobility, telco, FMCG such as Maggi/Nestlé CI, banks) and from **hotels**.
- The ministry is most likely to move on protocol and access, and because the proposition amplifies *its own* festival.
- We should **check her existing brand deals for conflicts** (for example if she already has a telco or mobility partner) before pitching any category.
