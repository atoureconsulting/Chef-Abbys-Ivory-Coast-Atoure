## Proposed: *"L'Heure du Goûter"* with the Fondation Olivia Yacé

- **Who:** Chef Abbys + **Olivia Yacé** (Miss Côte d'Ivoire 2021, tourism ambassador).
- **The foundation:** created in 2025. It funds girls' schooling, school kits and canteens. On 14 September 2026 it gave **6.6M FCFA** to the Hermann Gmeiner school at SOS Village d'Abobo.
- **Format:** a sweet-snack masterclass at 4pm (*le goûter*, Ivorian children's snack time), called **"same snack, two names"**:
  - The chefs demo **gbofloto vs bofrot**.
  - The kids make **dêguê cups and bissap**, which need no heat.
  - Optional: **Maison Mandjou** (Ahoua Touré) co-creates an "Abby × Olivia" biscuit with the kids.
- **Slot:** team's Day 1, 15:30–17:30 (currently rest time).
- **Patron:** **Ministry of Women, Family & Children** (Minister Nassénéba Touré). Tourism (Siandou Fofana) and Culture (Françoise Remarck) are invited.
