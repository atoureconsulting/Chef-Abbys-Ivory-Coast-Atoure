01 · The idea

She has tasted Abidjan.
Now she meets the people who feed it.

Her last visit was street food and learning attiéké. This stop meets the cocoa growers and chocolatiers, the 16,000 women behind Attiéké des Lagunes, the children at a school lunch, and the chefs and stars who carry Ivorian food to the world. It will be the most educational stop on the tour, and three days after the Big Street Feast it continues her Zero Hunger cause.

02 · Why Côte d'Ivoire, why now
HookWhy it works on camera
World #1 in cocoa (~40% of supply), yet few Ivorians eat Ivorian chocolateA paradox made for short-form video, filmed in harvest season (main crop opens in October)
Cocoa traceability mandatory since 1 Sept 2026, ahead of the EU deforestation law (Jan 2027)A timely, educational "why this matters"
Attiéké des Lagunes won protected-origin status (Dabou, March 2026): 16,000 women, 167 villages, ~100 bn FCFAWomen's economic power and heritage; completes her earlier attiéké content rather than repeating it
Festival de la Cuisine Ivoirienne (Ministry of Tourism) ends in Kong on 17 OctThe ministry has a fresh campaign to amplify, and its newly crowned laureates cook with her
The Ghana–Côte d'Ivoire rivalry: football, attiéké vs gariBuilt-in engagement from both audiences

04 · The three days

Three days, three episodes

Mon 19 Oct
The Bean
Cocoa → chocolate
ArrivalProtocol welcome at FHB Airport: fast-track, a pagne welcome kit, fresh bissap and ginger juice.
AfternoonBean-to-bar at Le Chocolatier Ivoirien with Axel-Emmanuel Gbaou, the ex-banker whose chocolate was named "best in the world" (2022) and who has trained 2,000+ women in cocoa villages. She makes a Ghana × Côte d'Ivoire bar with the world's #1 and #2 cocoa.
EveningWelcome dinner with a leading Ivorian chef (target: Charlie Koffi, Villa Alfira) with ~8 tourism, culture and foundation stakeholders.
UpgradeCocoa farm visit (Azaguié / Abbé-Bégnini, ~1 h) in the late morning, for a full farm-to-bar day.
Tue 20 Oct
The Lagoon
Attiéké, women & a school lunch
MorningThe women of Attiéké des Lagunes, Dabou: cassava → pressing → fermenting → steaming, the economics, and the label that fights fakes. She cooks as a student, not a demonstrator.
Midday"From the lagoon to the lunchbox." Chef Abbys, the attiéké women and school cooks make lunch for ~400 pupils. Target: a Fondation Magic System school (Jacqueville, 2026). Short talk: food as a career.
AfternoonMarket supply stop (Adjamé / Treichville): where does Abidjan's food come from?
EveningMaquis night: garba, alloco, braised fish and live zouglou with Ivorian food creators.
Wed 21 Oct
The Table
Culture, stars & the cook-off
MorningMusée des Civilisations de Côte d'Ivoire: food rituals and the Akan roots Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire share.
Late AMMinistry courtesy call (Tourism & Leisure), linked to the Festival de la Cuisine Ivoirienne.
★ AfternoonGhana × Côte d'Ivoire: the Cook-Off. Chef Abbys + FCI 2026 laureates + a celebrity guest judge (targets: A'Salfo; Didier Drogba as the stretch ask) + 2–3 Ivorian food creators. Attiéké vs gari. Kedjenou vs light soup.
UpgradeCASOF × Abidjan masterclass: Chef Abbys School of Food teaches 20–30 young Ivorian cooks and creators. Then a sunset pinasse (boat) on the Ébrié lagoon.

Thu 22 Oct: airport protocol, then Air Côte d'Ivoire nonstop to Monrovia (~1 h 30); flight days to be confirmed. Every day keeps a free block for her team's own filming, and every outdoor segment has a covered backup (short rainy season).

06 · Funding approach

With four weeks' lead time and many 2026 budgets already committed, we ask for in-kind support first and cash second. The fastest decisions come from hotels and private brands with local marketing teams (mobility, telco, FMCG, banks). The Ministry of Tourism is most likely to support protocol and access, because the programme amplifies its own festival. We will clear every category against Chef Abbys' existing brand deals before pitching.
